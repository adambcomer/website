These photographs are from an early winter hike that I did in Lakewood, Colorado. What I liked the
most about this time of year is the dark colors from the deep blues to the pine greens.

During this this trip, I experimented with up close shots of the subjects that give the pine needles
and dried flowers a dreamy look.
